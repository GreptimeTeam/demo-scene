Learned from Sparse Satellite Observations. https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13941

Exact source URLs, attributes, selected regions and times are retained in
user-generated data/cache/*.json and data/run-*.json.

Modifications: selected time intervals and regional bounding boxes; selected
SatWeave's 11.2 micrometer band; converted precipitation from m/h to mm/h;
normalized longitudes; flattened grids to table rows; represented missing
values as SQL NULL; performed SQL joins and display-only spatial aggregations.
